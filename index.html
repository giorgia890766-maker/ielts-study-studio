主题：城市绿地与身心健康
约 3 分钟
In many cities, public parks are no longer seen as decorative spaces alone. Researchers and urban planners increasingly recognise their role in supporting physical and mental health. A short walk among trees can offer a break from traffic noise and crowded streets.\n\nGreen spaces may also encourage people to be more active. When parks are safe, accessible and well maintained, residents are more likely to walk, run or meet friends outdoors. These everyday activities can strengthen social connections as well as improve fitness.\n\nHowever, the benefits are not shared equally in every neighbourhood. Some communities have large parks nearby, while others have few pleasant outdoor spaces. For this reason, city planners need to consider not only how much green space exists, but also who can reach it easily. Small gardens, tree-lined streets and community planting projects can all contribute to healthier urban environments.
重点词汇
decorative 装饰性的 · accessible 易到达的
maintained 得到维护的 · neighbourhood 街区
contribute to 有助于 · urban environment 城市环境
